# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone is welcome to participate in `@cravingmaker/eslint-config`, regardless
of background, identity, disability, or experience. We expect participants to
treat one another with care and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Keep feedback constructive and focused on the work.
- Consider different perspectives and communication styles.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Credit others' contributions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, impersonation, and sharing private information without permission
are prohibited.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review is
also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy covers issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to
**[cravingmaker.id@gmail.com](mailto:cravingmaker.id@gmail.com)**. Include relevant
links, context, and any details you feel comfortable sharing. Keep sensitive
information out of public issues.

The project maintainer, **@cravingmaker**, handles reports and limits disclosure
to what is necessary to review and address them.

## Responding to violations

The maintainer will consider the available evidence, severity, and any repeated
behavior. Responses may include removing content, issuing a warning, temporarily
restricting participation, or permanently excluding someone.

Serious incidents may require immediate restrictions. Where appropriate, the
response will explain expectations and conditions for returning.
